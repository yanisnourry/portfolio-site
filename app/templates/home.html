Market Data Pipeline

In production

Async ingestion of OHLCV candles from Binance into TimescaleDB, served by a FastAPI REST and WebSocket API, with a Streamlit dashboard on top. Anomalies (gaps, outliers, inconsistent candles) are flagged, never silently dropped, and every write is an idempotent upsert.

Vectorized Backtester

Engine + live demo

An engine that measures a strategy honestly: the position on each bar comes from the previous bar's signal, so no decision sees its own close, and every position change pays fees and slippage. There is one demo strategy, a plain SMA crossover: a test vehicle for the engine, not an edge. A small service re-runs it every hour on the pipeline's latest data.

Infrastructure

Self-hosted k3s

A single-node k3s cluster on a mini PC. Traffic comes in through a Cloudflare Tunnel, so no inbound port is open on the machine, and Traefik routes it from there. Each service ships as a Helm chart pinning an image that CI built and pushed to GHCR, by commit sha. The host is locked down with ufw on IPv4 and IPv6 and unattended security upgrades. This site runs there too.
